📖 Reading｜閱讀偵探

閱讀短文，再選出最符合文章內容的答案。考點：同義替換與主旨理解。

Passage

Many universities are introducing peer-mentoring programmes to help first-year students adapt to campus life. Instead of relying only on formal lectures, new students can talk to experienced students about study routines, social activities and academic challenges. Research suggests that these informal conversations may increase students' sense of belonging. However, the success of such programmes depends on regular training and clear expectations for mentors.

1. What is the main purpose of peer-mentoring programmes?
2. What may improve students' sense of belonging?

🗣️ Speaking｜口說挑戰

Part 1 + Part 2 延伸練習。建議先口頭回答，再用錄音功能回聽（可使用手機或電腦內建錄音工具）。

Part 1

What do you enjoy most about being a university student?

Do you prefer studying alone or with other people? Why?

Part 2｜Cue Card

Describe a skill you would like to improve.

You should say:

  • what the skill is
  • why you want to improve it
  • how you plan to practise it
  • and explain how this skill may help you in the future
口說自我評估（每項 1–5 分）
完成得很好！回聽時特別注意：是否有直接回答問題、是否能延伸理由與例子，以及文法錯誤是否影響理解。

🧠 Vocabulary｜字彙補給站

不要只背中文意思，試著把單字放進自己的句子。

adapt to適應｜Students need time to adapt to university life.
significant顯著的、重要的｜There has been a significant improvement.
participate in參與｜Students should participate in group activities.
beneficial有益的｜Volunteering can be beneficial to young people.
approach方法、途徑｜Teachers may use different approaches.
increasingly日益地｜Technology is increasingly used in education.
小測驗：選出最適合的字

Group discussions can be ______ to students because they encourage the exchange of ideas.
